DocuChat Evidence

    Uploaded text is stored as vectors in Pinecone and sent to Google (Gemini) to answer questions. On the free tier Google may use inputs to improve its products. Do not upload confidential documents. Sessions are deleted after 24 hours.

    Ask a question about the 30 demo arXiv papers. Every answer cites the file and page it used. If the papers do not contain the answer, the app says so.
